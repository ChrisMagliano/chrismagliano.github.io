<section class="home-section">

<p class="home-section-label">ABOUT ME</p>

<h2>From data to action.</h2>

<p>
My work covers the analytics lifecycle: from <strong>tracking and data collection</strong>
to analysis, experimentation, visualization and communication of insights.
</p>

<p>
I work with complex customer journeys, combining product and marketing data to identify
friction points, understand user behaviour and support product and business decisions.
</p>

<p>
What I enjoy most is going beyond <em>what happened</em> to understand
<strong>why it happened, what we can learn from it, and what we should do next.</strong>
</p>

<p class="home-section-label">MY BACKGROUND</p>

<h2>Different questions. Same scientific mindset.</h2>

<h3>From Astrophysics to Product Analytics 🔭</h3>

<p>
Before moving into Product Analytics, I completed a <strong>PhD in Astrophysics
at the University of Naples Federico II</strong>, focusing primarily on exoplanets
and the scientific exploitation of the PLATO space mission.
</p>

<p>
Research taught me how to approach ambiguous problems, work with complex datasets,
formulate and test hypotheses, and communicate results clearly.
</p>

<p>
The subject changed from <strong>planets and stars to users and digital products</strong>,
but the underlying process remains remarkably similar:
observe, formulate hypotheses, analyze data, test and learn.
</p>
