Learn the concepts

What is compound interest?

Returns can themselves earn future returns. For a lump sum, FV = PV × (1 + r/m)^(m×t), where r is the annual nominal rate, m the compounding periods per year, and t the years.

How are monthly contributions modeled?

Each month, the current balance grows by the equivalent monthly return and then the monthly contribution is added. This represents end-of-month payments, not beginning-of-month deposits.

What does inflation-adjusted value mean?

The projected nominal balance is divided by (1 + inflation rate)^years, expressing its purchasing power in today's euros. *The real-gain comparison discounts each historical contribution to today's purchasing-power basis at its payment date; it is a simplified illustrative measure, not an investment performance metric.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. This is a deterministic scenario, not a forecast or investment recommendation. Actual markets fluctuate, and taxes, fees, volatility, and contribution timing can materially change outcomes.
